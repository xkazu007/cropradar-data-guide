Ground truth — Moroccan parcels (Figshare)

Ground truth — Moroccan parcels (Figshare)

14 cropsWheat (2,854 parcels)

10,383 field polygons from six Moroccan zones (Doukkala, Gharb, El Haouz, Tadla, Souss, Settat), visited between December 2023 and January 2025. Crops: wheat is the star with **2,854 parcels**, plus corn, alfalfa, potatoes, beets, peas, peanut, onions, tomatoes, sugarcane, pumpkins, fava beans, melons, watermelon, tree crops (argan), bare soil and weeds. Each field carries its irrigation type, visit date, zone and the filename of a field photo (photos live in a 16 GB companion archive we haven't downloaded yet). CC BY 4.0, fully open. This is our ground truth — the answer key everything else gets validated against.

Wheat disease images

LWDCD2020

Wheat

about 8,926 field photos of wheat with leaf rust, Septoria, leaf smut, wheat streak, powdery mildew, and healthy plants. Wheat only. Shared via a Google Drive link from a third-party GitHub README; license ⚠️ unverified — don't treat as production-safe until confirmed. Good training material for a wheat disease classifier.

Wheat Leaf Dataset (Mendeley Data)

407 leaf photos labeled by pathologists: stripe rust (208), healthy (102), Septoria (97), taken on a wheat farm in Ethiopia in uncontrolled field conditions. Wheat only. Publicly viewable on Mendeley Data (a free login may be needed to download); license ⚠️ undeclared. Rusts and Septoria hit Moroccan wheat too, so it's a usable benchmark.

Barley disease images

MaBaKI

Barley

scanned barley kernel photos: 6 two-row malting barley varieties grown in controlled plots in Poland, scanned at 400 dpi (each raw image holds 450–480 kernels), giving over 10,000 segmented single-kernel images per variety, plus a healthy-vs-defective collection. Barley only. Publicly published; license ⚠️ statement not surfaced — verify before ingesting. Use case: variety identification and grain quality, not field disease.

Verify license

Barley hyperspectral disease set (Lundstedt et al.)

Barley

hyperspectral signatures of barley infected with net blotch, spot blotch, stem rust, powdery mildew, scald, head blight, loose smut, common root rot, and Fusarium head blight. Barley only. Request-only from the authors — not public. Listed because net blotch, rusts and powdery mildew matter for Moroccan barley; skip for now.

Not public

Ethiopian barley disease study

Barley

farm photos in 3 classes (healthy leaf, net blotch, leaf scald), labeled with agronomist help; their EfficientNetB0 reached 97% in the experiment. Malting barley only. Paper-only: no dataset download found. A candidate for a polite author email later.

Wheat weed images

MMIDDWF

WheatWeeds

2,489 wheat-field photos of wheat, broadleaf weeds and grass weeds in RGB *and* depth, captured from nine viewpoints for 3D weed detection. Wheat + weeds. The paper is CC BY; the dataset/repo license ⚠️ needs verification. Origin: China.

WheatWeeds

960 wheat/weed photos with bounding boxes plus 100,032 unlabeled photos, from 8 experimental fields with varying drill rates, fertilizer and herbicide treatments. Wheat + weeds. CC BY-NC 4.0, fully open via a GitHub wrapper that downloads from Zenodo. Note the NC clause blocks commercial reuse.

BarleyWeeds

BAWSeg

BarleyWeeds

a large-scale UAV benchmark for barley weed semantic segmentation: repeated RTK drone surveys over 2 commercial rainfed barley paddocks in West Australia across 4 growing seasons (2020–2023), flown with a DJI Phantom 4 Multispectral (blue/green/red/red-edge/NIR plus RGB), with radiometric calibration panels. Barley + weeds. Needs a free IEEE DataPort account; license ⚠️ not surfaced. Closest agronomic match to Moroccan barley (rainfed Mediterranean-type system).

BarleyWeeds

custom UAV with a MicaSense RedEdge 3 plus a thermal camera over barley at the 4-leaf stage; 544 photos with multispectral + NDVI + thermal + canopy-height channels. Barley + weeds. Paper-only — the dataset was never released.

Soil

SoilGrids (ISRIC)

All crops

global 250 m predictions of soil pH, organic carbon, clay/sand/silt, bulk density, CEC, nitrogen, carbon stocks and water variables at 6 depth intervals. All crops (wheat and barley included). CC BY 4.0, fully open: GeoTIFF/COG download, WMS, and a point-query REST API. Full Morocco coverage — our soil backbone.

Open

Yield tables

FAOSTAT (QCL)

WheatBarley+170 crops

country × crop × year tables of production (tonnes), area harvested (ha) and yield (kg/ha), 1961–2024, covering wheat and barley among 170+ crops and 244 countries. Fully open CSV; license CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Our single most important Morocco series: national wheat and barley yield history.

Open

Crop-yield prediction dataset (Kaggle)

Wheat

28,242 rows × 7 columns derived from FAO/World Bank data: country, crop, year (1990–2013), yield (hg/ha), annual rainfall, pesticide tonnes, average temperature; 101 countries, 10 crops including wheat (barley not among the 10). Needs a Kaggle login; license ⚠️ unverified. Quick regression baseline; prefer FAOSTAT direct.

GWHD 2021

Wheat

field photos with wheat-head annotations: 275,468 heads across train/val/test splits of 3,657 / 1,476 / 1,382 photos, for counting and detection — a yield proxy. Wheat only. Zenodo download cited in the project; verify the record's license field. Global collection.

CropHarvest

CC BY-SA 4.0, fully open on Zenodo. Good ML-ready benchmark for crop-type classification from satellite. *(Parked 2026-10-05: satellite data out of scope for now.)*

Weather & climate

NASA POWER

All crops

daily agro-climatology for any coordinate: precipitation, temperature (mean/max/min), humidity, solar radiation, evapotranspiration, 1981–present. All crops. Fully open REST API, no key needed. Morocco-wide usable.

Open

Open-Meteo

All crops

hourly temperature, humidity, radiation, precipitation and reference ET for any coordinates back to 1940, plus a 16-day forecast endpoint. All crops. Free for non-commercial use (CC BY 4.0 attribution required); no API key. High Morocco relevance.

CHIRPS

All crops

daily precipitation at 0.05° (~5 km), 1981–near present, a satellite + station blend designed for drought monitoring in data-sparse regions. All crops. Free and open download (also on Google Earth Engine). The standard rainfall product for African ag work.

Open

Satellite crop maps

WorldCereal (ESA)

WheatBarley (+ rye)

global 10 m crop-type maps from Sentinel-1/2; 2021 products map wheat separately and group wheat + barley + rye as cereals; Phase II plans separate wheat and barley products. Fully open via the project's Zenodo repository and Google Earth Engine. Full Morocco coverage — directly usable for winter-cereal season maps.

Open

BreizhCrops

WheatBarley

Sentinel-2 time series for crop-type mapping in Brittany, France, with explicit wheat and barley labels (~608,000 labeled observations; benchmark subset 13,850 wheat, 4,982 barley). Verify license and download on the repo. Method-transfer value (temporal CNN baselines), not Moroccan fields.

Verify license

Kazakhstan UAV multispectral

WheatBarley

5 drone flight sessions (7,200–8,300 raw aerial photos each, 15–17.7 GB per session) plus processed orthomosaics per crop: barley 12.7 GB (59 plots), wheat 11.8 GB (60 plots), with a plot metadata table (variety, sowing rate, sowing date). Wheat & barley. About 80 GB total; license and download mechanism ⚠️ need verification. A genuine paired wheat/barley UAV resource for pre-training.

Pests

Wheat aphid loads (Dryad)

Wheat

tabular data from 120 studies (China + Europe, 1970–2017): wheat aphid loads with temperature, pesticide use, fertilization, land use and natural-enemy covariates. Wheat only. Direct Dryad download, usually no account; Dryad's default is typically CC0 — ⚠️ verify on the record. Evidence base for pest-pressure modeling.

Verify license

ICARDA RWA trials (MELDATA)

WheatBarley (host)

experimental CSVs: bread-wheat evaluation for Russian wheat aphid resistance, 2002 & 2003 experiments, with data dictionaries. Wheat (and the Russian wheat aphid also attacks barley — host overlap). CC-BY-NC-4.0; some files need a MELDATA login, some are request-access. High Morocco relevance: RWA is a major North African wheat/barley pest and ICARDA works in Morocco.

Wheat
